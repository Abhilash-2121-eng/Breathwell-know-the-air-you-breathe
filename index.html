Behind the numbers

How polluted air is made

Most of what the index measures starts as simple reactions in the atmosphere.

Ground-level ozone

NO₂ + sunlight → NO + O
O + O₂ → O₃

Ozone isn’t emitted directly. Sunlight splits nitrogen dioxide from vehicles, and the freed oxygen atom joins an O₂ molecule. That’s why ozone peaks on hot afternoons.

Fine particles

SO₂ + OH· → HSO₃·
→ H₂SO₄ (sulphate aerosol)

PM2.5 is smaller than 2.5 µm, small enough to reach the bloodstream. Part of it forms in the air when gases like SO₂ and NOₓ condense into tiny particles.

Acid rain

SO₂ + ½O₂ + H₂O → H₂SO₄
2NO₂ + H₂O → HNO₃ + HNO₂

Sulphur and nitrogen oxides dissolve in cloud water and lower its pH, harming soil, water bodies and old buildings.

Take action

Small habits, cleaner air

Protecting yourself and cutting emissions both matter.

Check before you go

Look at the AQI before outdoor plans. Ventilate your home when the index is lowest, and use a chimney or exhaust fan while cooking.

Mask up properly

A well-fitted N95 or FFP2 mask filters fine particles. Cloth and surgical masks let most PM2.5 through.

Clean the air indoors

A HEPA purifier sized for the room helps on bad days. Avoid incense, mosquito coils and indoor smoking.

Move differently

Walk, cycle, carpool or take public transport. Fewer engines on the road means less NO₂ and PM2.5.

Never burn waste

Burning leaves, plastic or crop residue releases particles and toxic gases. Compost or hand it to collection services.

Grow green cover

Trees trap dust and shade streets, lowering the heat that drives ozone formation.

Questions

Good to know

What does the AQI actually measure?

It converts the concentrations of up to eight pollutants into a single number from 0 to 500. The pollutant with the worst sub-index decides the final value.

Why is air often worse in winter?

Cold, still air near the ground traps pollutants under a warmer layer, which is called a temperature inversion. People also burn more fuel for heat.

Do cloth masks protect against smog?

They stop large droplets but let most fine particles through. A snug N95 is designed to filter at least 95% of them.

Is indoor air always cleaner?

Usually for dust and soot, but cooking smoke, incense and cleaning sprays can make indoor air worse than the street outside.
